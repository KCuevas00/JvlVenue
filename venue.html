Welcome to JVL Venue

An Intimate Reception Salon in Plano, IL

JVL Venue is a private salon and reception hall located in Plano, IL, thoughtfully crafted to give families, friends, and organizations an intimate and welcoming setting for memorable celebrations of up to 75 guests.

Whether you are hosting a birthday party, quinceañera, baptism, communion, baby shower, or family celebration, our venue provides a clean, versatile canvas. Every hall rental comes standard with tables, chairs, and tablecloths. Custom decoration styling is also available for an extra price upon request.

Rental Information

Venue Guidelines & Important Notes

Rental Hours: Standard evening rental schedule runs from 5:00 PM to 11:30 PM. Inquire to coordinate date availability and entry setup times.

Included Amenities: Every hall rental includes tables, chairs, and tablecloths. Custom decoration setups (backdrops, balloon arches) are available for an extra price upon request.

Guest Capacity: Our salon comfortably accommodates up to 75 guests maximum for safety and fire code compliance.

Pricing & Rates: In accordance with our venue policy, fixed rates are not listed publicly. Quotes are tailored upon inquiry based on your date, occasion, and specific setup requirements.

Outside Catering & Refreshments: Hosts are welcome to bring outside catering, homemade dishes, celebration cakes, and refreshments.

Rental Contract & Rules: Official venue guidelines, deposit details, and rental terms are set forth in the formal rental contract provided upon booking inquiry.

Questions or Looking to Check a Date?

Contact us directly to discuss your desired celebration date and schedule a walkthrough.
