Welcome to JVL Venue

An Intimate Reception Salon in Plano, IL

JVL Venue is a private salon and reception hall located in Plano, IL, thoughtfully set up to host gatherings and celebrations for up to 75 guests. Standard rental hours run from 5:00 PM to 11:30 PM, providing an intimate, welcoming setting where you can celebrate comfortably.

Every hall rental includes tables, chairs, and tablecloths. Whether you are hosting a birthday party, family celebration, shower, or private reception, we offer a versatile space where you can personalize the atmosphere or request our custom decoration packages for an additional fee.
